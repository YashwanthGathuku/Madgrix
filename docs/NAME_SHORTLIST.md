# Public Name — Decision Record

**Status: DECIDED 2026-10-01 — the product is Madgrix.**
Public repository: `github.com/YashwanthGathuku/Madgrix` (MIT).

Collision screening for "Madgrix" (web search, 2026-10-01): **no exact
"Madgrix" software product found.** Near-misses, both distinct in spelling
and domain:
- **MADRIX** — LED lighting-control software by inoage GmbH (registered
  trademark, madrix.com). Different spelling, different industry.
- **Madgicx** — AI ad-management platform (madgicx.com). Different spelling,
  different industry.

Screening verdicts are not legal opinions — a full trademark search
(USPTO/EUIPO/UKIPO) and legal review are still required before launch, and
domain availability was not checked.

"Seam" was rejected as the product name (crowded namespace: an existing
developer platform called Seam, a SEAM semantic-governance product for AI
agents, zer07 Labs' "Seam", and a US SEAM trademark application covering
downloadable software). It survives only as the internal working codename
and as the in-architecture term **"Verdict Seam"**.
