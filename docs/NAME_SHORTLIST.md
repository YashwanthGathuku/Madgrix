# Public Name Shortlist

**Status: undecided.** Nothing in the repo has been renamed and nothing is publicly
branded — the folder stays `seam/` as an internal codename, and "Verdict Seam" is
used only as in-architecture terminology. "Seam" itself is rejected as a product name
(crowded namespace, trademark risk).

Collision screening was done via web search on 2026-10-01 (product, company, and
trademark databases). These are **screening verdicts, not legal opinions** — a full
trademark search and legal review are still required before launch, and domain
availability was not checked.

## Candidates (8)

1. **Placet** — *Clean-ish.* No direct software product found; noisy near-matches are
   Placer.ai (location analytics), Palette, and Plat.AI — all distinct names.
2. **Verilo** — *Clean-ish.* No direct product found; nearby marks are Veriker and
   Veraify in the AI/security space — adjacent, not colliding.
3. **Halden** — *Clean-ish.* No direct software product found; noise includes the
   Hadean cloud platform, the Holden Adaptive Platform, and a Halden Miller Framer
   template — none in this space.
4. **Avouch** — *Clean-ish.* No direct software product found; the only company hit is
   the unrelated Avouch Infoservices Pvt Ltd.
5. **Ordal** — *Clean-ish.* No direct product found; the near-match is Ordinal AI (a
   different word, an AI gov assistant).
6. **Meritum** — *Weak but usable.* No direct product found; the Merit\* neighborhood
   is crowded (Meridian, MeritFinder, Meritsoft, iMerit) — expect brand noise.
7. **Votum** — *Moderate risk.* US VOTUM marks were filed by Votum Enterprises LLC
   (90458029, 90568505 — both abandoned Jan 2022) and Lautsprecher Teufel GmbH
   (79110130 — cancelled 2021); no live agent product, but residual common-law risk
   from prior use.
8. **Trier** — *Weak.* No product named Trier found; noise is a surname (ModelOp's CEO)
   and the German city — a city name makes a mediocre brand.

## Rejected during screening

Keryx, Cairn, Rill, Fiducia, Vindex, Aedile, Assent, Probat, Countersign, Keur, and
Fidem were all dropped for direct product collisions or registered marks in software
classes (details in the 2026-10-01 screening notes).

## Before choosing

- [ ] Full trademark search (USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO at minimum) on the finalists.
- [ ] Legal review of the top pick.
- [ ] Domain + package-registry availability.
- [ ] Then: rename in the clean competition repo only — never in this working tree.
